Three AI agent teams turned a Python GPT into bit-exact RTL in under twenty hours, and one token can be traced through every layer

Every word an AI chat streams back is chosen one token at a time. This article follows one such token, the letter l in the generated name karilen, backwards from a chat window down to standard cells: through the generation loop, about 200 lines of Python, an integer model, a written spec, SystemVerilog, synthesis and verification.
